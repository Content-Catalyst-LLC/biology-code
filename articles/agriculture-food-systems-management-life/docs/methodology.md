# Methodology Notes

## Purpose

This repository demonstrates reproducible computational scaffolding for agriculture and food-system indicators.

## Core Indicators

The workflow models several concepts:

- crop yield
- water productivity
- nutrient use efficiency
- food-loss rate
- soil organic carbon change
- biodiversity-resilience index
- diet-diversity score
- provenance and reproducibility

## Yield

yield_t_ha =
  production_tonnes / area_hectares

## Water Productivity

water_productivity =
  production_tonnes / water_used_m3

## Nutrient Use Efficiency

nutrient_use_efficiency =
  nutrient_harvested_kg / nutrient_input_kg

## Food-Loss Rate

food_loss_rate =
  food_lost_tonnes / production_tonnes

## Soil Organic Carbon Change

delta_soc =
  soc_t1 - soc_t0

## Biodiversity-Resilience Index

resilience_index =
  0.35 * crop_diversity
  + 0.35 * soil_biological_function
  + 0.30 * landscape_heterogeneity

## Scientific Caution

These are educational models. They are not farm-management prescriptions, official sustainability indicators, or food-security forecasts.
