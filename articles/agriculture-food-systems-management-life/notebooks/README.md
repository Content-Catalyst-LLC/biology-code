# Notebook Scaffold

This folder contains a minimal notebook scaffold for the article workflow.

Suggested notebook sections:

1. Load production-system metadata.
2. Calculate yield, water productivity, nutrient efficiency, and food-loss rates.
3. Score biodiversity and resilience.
4. Calculate soil organic carbon change.
5. Score diet diversity.
6. Account for food loss and waste by stage.
7. Generate provenance manifest.
8. Interpret results with ecological, nutrition, public-health, and justice caution.
