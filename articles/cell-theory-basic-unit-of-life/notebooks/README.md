# Notebook Workflow

The notebook scaffold demonstrates how to connect cell-growth modeling, viability decay, membrane flux, cell-cycle compartments, imaging features, condition scoring, and provenance documentation.

Suggested notebook sections:

1. Load cell-count observations and fit growth rates.
2. Estimate doubling times by condition.
3. Simulate logistic growth under constraints.
4. Fit viability-loss rates and estimate half-life.
5. Calculate membrane flux under concentration gradients.
6. Simulate simplified cell-cycle compartments.
7. Summarize imaging features.
8. Score cell-condition examples.
9. Record provenance and limitations.

The included `.ipynb` file is intentionally minimal so it can be opened and expanded in JupyterLab or VS Code.
