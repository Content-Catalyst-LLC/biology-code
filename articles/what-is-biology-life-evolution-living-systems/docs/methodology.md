# Methodology Notes

## Purpose

The computational examples formalize several foundational biological ideas: growth, constraint, inheritance, biodiversity, sequence comparison, and biological organization across scales.

## Exponential Growth

N(t) = N0 exp(r t)

## Logistic Growth

dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K)

## Hardy-Weinberg Expectations

p + q = 1

p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1

## Shannon Diversity

H = -sum p_i log(p_i)

where p_i is relative abundance of taxon i.

## Sequence Similarity

S = 1 - d/L

where d is aligned mismatch count and L is alignment length.

## Interpretation

These workflows are educational and methodological scaffolds. They do not replace full ecological modeling, advanced population genetics, sequence alignment, phylogenetic inference, clinical interpretation, or validated biodiversity monitoring.
