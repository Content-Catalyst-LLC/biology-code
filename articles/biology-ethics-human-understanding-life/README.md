# Biology, Ethics, and the Human Understanding of Life

This article repository supports the Biology knowledge-series article:

Biology, Ethics, and the Human Understanding of Life

The repository provides reproducible computational scaffolding for transparent ethical-review workflows in biology. It includes synthetic biological research projects, ethical-review dimensions, consent-completeness indicators, justice-adjusted benefit calculations, ecological-risk scoring, reversibility analysis, governance notes, SQL provenance structures, notebook scaffolding, and cross-language examples in Python, R, Julia, Fortran, Rust, Go, C, and C++.

No university affiliation is implied by this repository. The design goal is advanced reproducible computational rigor: clear assumptions, transparent ethical dimensions, provenance records, responsible-use notes, and reusable scientific workflow structure.

## Repository Structure

- python/ — ethical-review scoring, consent completeness, justice-adjusted benefit, ecological risk, provenance, and reporting
- r/ — consent completeness, ecological risk, and justice-adjusted benefit cross-checks
- julia/ — ethical-score numerical kernels
- fortran/ — legacy-style benefit-harm kernel
- rust/ — safe command-line ethical scenario ranking utility
- go/ — portable governance metadata summary helper
- c/ — compact ecological-risk kernel
- cpp/ — biological ethics scenario ranking example
- sql/ — projects, ethical dimensions, consent records, ecological risk, governance notes, and provenance
- docs/ — setup, methodology, validation, responsible use, and reproducibility notes
- data/ — synthetic biology ethics datasets
- notebooks/ — minimal notebook scaffold
- outputs/ — generated tables, reports, manifests, and figures

## Scientific and Ethical Focus

The workflows demonstrate:

1. Transparent ethical-review scoring.
2. Consent-completeness indicators.
3. Justice-adjusted benefit calculations.
4. Ecological-risk and reversibility scoring.
5. Governance metadata for biological research.
6. SQL-backed ethical-review provenance.
7. Cross-language numerical reproducibility.
8. Responsible interpretation of ethics-support tools.

These examples are educational and methodological scaffolds. They are not institutional review board tools, clinical ethics systems, regulatory approvals, animal-care approvals, biodiversity-governance systems, consent platforms, or automated moral decision systems. Real ethical decisions require institutional review, human deliberation, legal compliance, community participation, domain expertise, and accountable governance.
