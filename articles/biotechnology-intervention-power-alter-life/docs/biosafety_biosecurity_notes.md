# Biosafety and Biosecurity Notes

Responsible biotechnology requires both biosafety and biosecurity.

## Biosafety

Biosafety focuses on preventing accidental exposure, release, contamination, or harm. It includes containment, procedures, training, institutional review, waste handling, incident response, and appropriate facility design.

## Biosecurity

Biosecurity focuses on preventing misuse, theft, diversion, or malicious application. It includes access control, sequence screening, inventory management, personnel reliability, cyberbiosecurity, and dual-use review.

## Scope

These notes are educational. They do not replace institutional biosafety committee review, legal compliance, clinical regulatory review, environmental assessment, or national/international biosecurity requirements.
