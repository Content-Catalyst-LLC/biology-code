# Validation Notes

## Epidemiological Modeling

- Reported cases are not the same as true infections.
- Testing, reporting, and surveillance systems can change over time.
- SIR and SEIR models often assume homogeneous mixing unless extended.
- Rt estimates require careful treatment of delays, generation intervals, and uncertainty.
- Scenario results are conditional, not predictions.
- Interventions should be modeled through explicit mechanisms.
- Forecast validation should use data not used for fitting.

## Data Governance

- Real epidemiological data may involve privacy risks.
- Small-area data can risk re-identification.
- Public communication should avoid stigmatizing groups or places.
- Public-health interpretation requires domain review.

## Limitations

The examples do not implement operational outbreak analytics, Bayesian inference, renewal-equation Rt estimation, agent-based simulation, spatial modeling, genomic epidemiology, age-structured contact matrices, vaccination dynamics, healthcare-capacity modeling, or official public-health forecasting.
