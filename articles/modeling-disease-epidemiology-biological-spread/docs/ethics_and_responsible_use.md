# Ethics and Responsible Use

This repository is educational.

Do not use these scripts for clinical guidance, public-health directives, outbreak response, risk scoring of individuals, surveillance of identifiable people, or operational forecasting.

Responsible epidemiological modeling requires:

- public-health expertise
- epidemiological review
- data-quality assessment
- privacy review
- uncertainty communication
- bias assessment
- community context
- transparent provenance
- appropriate governance

Models can support judgment, but they should not replace public-health expertise.
