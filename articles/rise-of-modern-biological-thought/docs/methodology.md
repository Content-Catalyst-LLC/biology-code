# Methodology Notes

## Purpose

The computational examples formalize several ideas that became central to modern biological thought: growth, constraint, heredity, allele-frequency change, molecular comparison, and reproducible biological data structures.

## Exponential Growth

N(t) = N0 exp(r t)

## Logistic Growth

dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K)

## Hardy-Weinberg Expectations

p + q = 1

p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1

## Selection Recurrence

p' = (p^2 wAA + p q wAa) / wbar

where:

wbar = p^2 wAA + 2 p q wAa + q^2 waa

## Sequence Similarity

S = 1 - d/L

where d is aligned mismatch count and L is alignment length.

## Interpretation

These workflows are simple computational scaffolds. They do not replace expert population genetics, phylogenetic inference, historical scholarship, molecular biology, or empirical validation.
