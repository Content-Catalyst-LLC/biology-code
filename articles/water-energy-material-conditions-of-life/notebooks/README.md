# Notebook Workflow

The notebook scaffold demonstrates how to connect osmotic pressure, water potential, homeostatic setpoint dynamics, growth fitting, oxygen limitation, energy allocation, material-condition scoring, and provenance documentation.

Suggested notebook sections:

1. Load solute conditions and estimate osmotic pressure.
2. Load water-potential components and calculate total water potential.
3. Simulate homeostatic setpoint return.
4. Fit exponential growth by condition.
5. Estimate oxygen-limited relative energy rate.
6. Summarize energy allocation fractions.
7. Score material-condition examples.
8. Record provenance and limitations.

The included `.ipynb` file is intentionally minimal so it can be opened and expanded in JupyterLab or VS Code.
