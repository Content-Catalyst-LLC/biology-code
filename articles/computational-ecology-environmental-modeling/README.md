# Computational Ecology and Environmental Modeling

This article repository supports the Biology knowledge-series article:

**Computational Ecology and Environmental Modeling**

This repository provides reproducible workflows for habitat suitability, patch occupancy, environmental stress scenarios, runoff scaffolds, validation metrics, provenance, SQL audit structures, notebook documentation, and cross-language scientific-computing scaffolding.

No university affiliation is implied by this repository. The design goal is advanced reproducible computational rigor: clear assumptions, modular code, transparent data structures, provenance, validation checks, uncertainty awareness, and reusable workflows.

## Repository Structure

- `python/` — primary workflows for habitat suitability, occupancy, environmental stress, runoff, validation, provenance, and report generation
- `r/` — ecology and environmental modeling cross-checks
- `julia/` — numerical ecology kernels
- `fortran/` — compact environmental modeling kernel
- `rust/` — safe command-line ecology summary utility
- `go/` — portable ecology summary helper
- `c/` — compact ecological modeling kernel
- `cpp/` — scenario-based ecological modeling implementation
- `sql/` — sites, environmental grid, scenarios, validation, workflow steps, artifacts, and provenance schema
- `docs/` — setup, methodology, validation, and reproducibility notes
- `data/` — synthetic reproducible ecological and environmental datasets
- `notebooks/` — notebook scaffold
- `outputs/` — generated tables, simulations, figures, and reports

## Scientific Focus

The workflows are designed to support transparent computational ecology and environmental modeling:

1. Estimate habitat suitability from environmental covariates.
2. Simulate patch-occupancy dynamics.
3. Compare climate-stress and restoration scenarios.
4. Calculate simple runoff scaffolds.
5. Validate model outputs against synthetic observations.
6. Record provenance and artifact checksums.
7. Store environmental modeling records in SQL.
8. Cross-check core calculations across multiple languages.

These examples are educational and methodological scaffolds. They are not regulatory environmental models, conservation-decision systems, hydrological engineering models, climate projections, or operational ecological forecasts. Real applications require domain-specific validation, statistical review, spatial-data review, stakeholder review where relevant, and appropriate governance.
