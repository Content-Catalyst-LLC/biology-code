# Responsible Use Notes

This repository is educational.

Do not use these scripts for:

- clinical diagnosis
- treatment decisions
- antimicrobial prescribing
- public-health forecasting
- cancer prognosis
- genetic counseling
- disease-risk scoring
- automated triage
- epidemiological policy decisions

Responsible evolutionary medicine requires clinical evidence, biological validation, public-health expertise, ethical interpretation, and attention to social and environmental determinants of health.
