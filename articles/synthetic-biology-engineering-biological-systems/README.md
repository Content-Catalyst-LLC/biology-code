# Synthetic Biology and the Engineering of Biological Systems

This article repository supports the Biology knowledge-series article:

Synthetic Biology and the Engineering of Biological Systems

The repository provides reproducible computational scaffolding for synthetic biology and engineering biology workflows. It includes synthetic design-build-test-learn records, construct metadata, biosensor performance calculations, host-burden scoring, metabolic-yield scaffolds, genetic-circuit dynamics, SQL provenance structures, notebook scaffolding, and cross-language examples in Python, R, Julia, Fortran, Rust, Go, C, and C++.

No university affiliation is implied by this repository. The design goal is advanced reproducible computational rigor: clear assumptions, transparent construct metadata, measurement-aware engineering scores, reproducible biological design records, biosafety-aware documentation, provenance records, and reusable scientific workflow structure.

## Repository Structure

- python/ — DBTL scoring, biosensor signal-to-noise, host burden, yield, provenance, and report generation
- r/ — host-burden and yield cross-checks
- julia/ — genetic-circuit and engineering-score kernels
- fortran/ — legacy-style circuit dynamics kernel
- rust/ — safe command-line design ranking utility
- go/ — portable construct metadata summary helper
- c/ — compact biosensor signal-to-noise kernel
- cpp/ — scenario ranking and synthetic-design assessment example
- sql/ — constructs, parts, chassis, measurements, DBTL cycles, provenance, and biosafety notes
- docs/ — setup, methodology, validation, biosafety, biosecurity, and reproducibility notes
- data/ — synthetic synthetic-biology design datasets
- notebooks/ — minimal notebook scaffold
- outputs/ — generated tables, reports, manifests, and figures

## Scientific Focus

The workflows demonstrate:

1. Synthetic biology design-build-test-learn records.
2. Engineering scores for synthetic constructs.
3. Biosensor signal-to-noise analysis.
4. Host-burden scoring.
5. Metabolic product yield calculations.
6. Genetic-circuit dynamic scaffolds.
7. SQL-backed construct and provenance metadata.
8. Cross-language numerical reproducibility.
9. Responsible interpretation of engineered biological systems.

These examples are educational and methodological scaffolds. They are not biosafety approvals, regulatory systems, validated biosensor designs, biomanufacturing process controls, ecological release models, or biosecurity screening systems. Real synthetic biology decisions require domain-specific validation, institutional review, legal compliance, biosafety assessment, sequence verification, measurement standards, and appropriate oversight.
